## Outcome
Extend the durable async-job/artifact model into a general durable inference
job contract covering all runtime families and pipelines: submitted via
`POST /jobs`, lifecycle `queued → running → succeeded | failed | cancelled`,
result-by-artifact-reference, resumable across restart, and family-typed with
no bypass of admission/capacity/fairshare/owner-priority.
