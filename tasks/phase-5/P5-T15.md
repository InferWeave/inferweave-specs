## Outcome
Granted standby slots are held against the granting site's measured envelope, expire on time since last progress, are revocable with notice under local owner priority, and return to the granting site's envelope on expiry or revocation.

## Required tests
- standby offered never exceeds the measured envelope
- expiry is evaluated on time since last progress, not elapsed time
- revocation honours the stated notice and returns slots to the envelope
- routing to an expired grant is rejected
