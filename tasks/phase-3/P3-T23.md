## Outcome
The autoscaler claims individual GPUs, rebalances one GPU per decision, holds each GPU for its model's dwell (configured, or max(300 s, 10 x measured p95 load)), never chooses a donor that would breach a model's healthy-instance floor or whose load cost is unmeasured, and persists loaded_at and swap intent on the claim.

## Required tests
- GPU inside dwell is never a donor and restart cannot shorten dwell
- derived dwell and per-model override
- model without measured load time is never a donor
- property: no decision sequence breaches an availability floor
- model at floor is freed only after another instance is healthy
- GpuDwell, AvailabilityFloor and UnmeasuredLoadCost holds carry their numbers
- existing autoscaler invariants still hold

## Operational evidence
On one two-GPU host, a least-recently-used runtime router with no dwell spawned model instances 52–82 times a day (2026-09-02 to -05), reloading weights and discarding the prompt cache on each alternation.
